Privacy Policy — EPA library

Last updated: 24 September 2026
App: EPA library (com.examproacademy.offline)

Hello!

We are Exam Pro Academy, the team behind the EPA library app and examproacademy.xyz.

EPA library helps health-sciences students in Tanzania study using question banks, notes and other study materials, including when they are offline.

This policy explains, in simple terms, what information the app collects, why we need it, how we use it, and what choices you have.

In short: We only collect information that is needed to provide the service. We do not sell your personal information, we do not display advertisements, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. Information we collect

Your KEY and registration information
When you register or use the app, we may collect information such as your KEY, name, course, level and subscription expiry date. We use this information to:

  • Sign you into the app
  • Confirm your account
  • Know which study content you are allowed to access
  • Manage your subscription or access period

Device information
The app collects your Android device ID. We use it to help prevent one KEY from being shared and used by many different people. We do not collect your phone number or your precise location through the app.

Technical information
Our server receives your IP address when the app connects to it. We use it for security and to limit misuse. If a KEY is used on a different device from the one it is registered to, we log the KEY, the device IDs and the IP address to help detect KEY sharing.

Questions and files you submit
If you use "Submit New Question", we receive the information you choose to send, such as the question text, subject or course, level, and any file you attach. We use these to review and, where appropriate, add useful material to our question banks for other students.

Messages sent to EPA Assistant
When you use the EPA Assistant, we receive the message you type and the name of the screen you are using. We store your questions and the assistant's answers together with your KEY so that we can improve the service and help you with problems. You can ask us to delete this history at any time.

2. Files on your phone

The Document Manager may need access to a folder that you choose yourself through the Android file picker. For example, you may choose a WhatsApp folder so that you can organise your study documents.

We do not automatically upload everything in that folder. Your files remain on your phone unless you specifically choose to send them, such as by attaching a file when submitting a question or starting a transfer or backup.

3. How we use and share your information

  • Information sent between the app and our server is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS.
  • Messages sent to the EPA Assistant are processed by third-party AI service providers acting on our behalf (currently Groq, Cerebras and Cloudflare Workers AI), only to generate a response. We do not send your KEY to these providers.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not display advertisements in the app.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising services in the app.
  • We may disclose information when required to do so by applicable law.

4. Data storage and retention

Study books, notes and your KEY may be stored inside the app on your phone. These are removed when you uninstall the app.

The app does not allow unencrypted communication with our server.

When you submit a question, the question text and any attached file are removed from your phone after they have reached the administrator.

Account information, questions you submit, and your EPA Assistant history may remain on our server while you are using the service, or until you ask us to delete them.

No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. However, we take reasonable measures to protect the information we handle.

5. Permissions used by the app

Internet and network access: allows the app to communicate with our server and check whether an internet connection is available.

File and folder access: provided through the Android file picker. The app can access only the file or folder that you choose.

6. Your rights and how to request deletion

You can contact us if you want to:

  • Know what personal information we have about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate
  • Ask us to delete your information

To request deletion, email us at the address in Section 9 with the subject "Delete my data" and include your KEY or the name you used when registering, so that we can identify your account.

After verifying your request, we will delete the requested data within 30 days, where applicable.

Uninstalling the app removes the information stored locally on your phone. It does not automatically delete information that may already be stored on our server, so please contact us if you also want that data deleted.

7. Children

EPA Mitihani is intended for students attending health colleges and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and, after verifying the situation, we will delete it.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. For important changes, we may also notify users through the app or another appropriate method.
